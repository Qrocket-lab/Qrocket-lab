## Selected Work

### Business Intelligence and Analytics

Building dashboards and reports for sales, operations, HR, and business performance.

One example came from an arcade rental business where the owner originally wanted to expand into another location. The POS analysis showed that the current operation still had stronger optimization opportunities.

A second finding showed that take-home rentals represented only around 12% of transaction volume while contributing 44.7% of revenue during the analysed period.

The lesson:

```text
Business Want != Business Need
Dashboard != Decision
Activity != Value
```

### Q-Equity Research Program

An evidence-first financial research system built around public company data.

```text
SEC EDGAR / XBRL
        |
        v
Raw Evidence
        |
        v
Normalization
        |
        v
PostgreSQL Research Layer
        |
        v
Validated Research Output
```

The system is designed around one principle:

> If the evidence is incomplete, the output should not pretend to be complete.

A market-observation research project focused on market conditions rather than prediction.
